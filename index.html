01 / MUSIC

BASS
CULTURE

Steady Riddim is a Mexican producer and sound system artist working mainly across dub and bass music, focused on the darker and underground side of the sound.

His musical language comes from the collision of Mexican street culture: cumbia's sonidero from the barrio, rap and hip hop, and a DIY approach to recording. His soundscape traces back to the roots of Jamaican sound system tradition and the bass cultures that followed it.

His tracks are produced to be rebuilt, dubbed and transformed through live performance, bringing space and movement to the riddim.

02 / LIVE DUB

REAL TIME
DELIVERY

Live Dub is not a DJ set. It is a real-time performance built from Steady Riddim's own productions, stems and multitracks.

Working with FL Studio and Akai MIDI-MIX controllers, tracks are taken apart and rebuilt in the moment: channels are muted, opened, filtered and pushed through delays, echoes, reverbs and other dub effects.

Rooted in the studio techniques of Jamaican dub pioneers such as King Tubby and Scientist, the performance treats the mix itself as an instrument.

No two sessions are exactly the same. The arrangement, effects and transitions emerge in real time, leaving space for improvisation, accident and the energy of the room.

RECORDS

Love, Sound & Distortion is an independent DIY netlabel, digital record label and creative platform built around bass culture and underground music.

The catalog moves between dub, bass music, hip hop, Latin bass, cumbia, experimental sounds and other independent productions, bringing together releases, collaborations and different stages of the journey.

More than a catalog, LSD Records works as an archive and a space for musical exchange: a place to release, document and connect sounds outside conventional industry structures.

04 / DUB IN THE STREETS

THEOUTSIDUB

TheOutsidub is the street-facing extension of the Live Dub practice: a nomadic DIY dub project with a portable, battery-powered setup. Built around FL Studio, Akai MIDI-MIX controllers and a melodica and focused on improvisation and real-time vibes delivery, creating a space of resistance for independent sound creation while busking to keep moving around. This whole project takes the same dub process of breaking down and rebuilding rhythms out of the studio and into the streets, public spaces and spontaneous sessions across the road during Steady's Europe Trip.

When Steady moved from Costa Rica to Europe, he started recording himself with his mobile phone, trying to document a little bit of dub in every city he passed through while travelling on a low-cost, minimal-budget setup. Between spring 2024 and February 2025, he filmed himself live dubbing some of the riddims he had been writing during those years, with February 2025 marking the last video of this first documented busking season. Without better equipment or a more practical way to film himself, new sessions have not been documented in the same way since, even though the street dub busking continues. What remains is the first season of TheOutsidub, recorded and edited by Steady and collected on YouTube, with the audio taken directly from the master output of FL Studio. This gives the series a hi-fi listening experience for people discovering the sessions from home, while keeping the raw street performance at the centre of the project.
