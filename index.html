04 / DUB IN THE STREETS

THEOUTSIDUB

A nomadic DIY dub project taking a portable battery-powered sound system into the streets.

Built around FL Studio, Akai MIDImix controllers and melodica, TheOutsidub becomes a mobile dub station for busking, improvisation and spontaneous sessions across Europe.

Sound, vibration and public space become a form of resistance, connection and independent sound system culture.
